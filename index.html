Concept only — these buttons do nothing.

What this is. A working concept for one process — carrier-facing dock appointment booking, with driver check-in and check-out captured at the gate. Built to show the workflow, not as production software. Nothing here connects to a real system.

Why this process. Ramp Enterprise WMS has no dock-scheduling module for inbound freight that doesn't arrive over EDI — a gap its own users raise publicly. That gap is why an Appointment/CSR is needed to answer phones, book doors, prepare tallies and key driver arrival and departure times back into the WMS by hand.

Every carrier name, reference, pallet count, temperature and dollar figure on this page is invented for demonstration. No real customer, shipment or operational data from Cold Solutions was used, and none of these figures should be read as a claim about their actual performance.
